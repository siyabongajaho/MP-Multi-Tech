    About Us

    Advanced Care for Your Vehicle's Electronics.

    At MP Multi-Tech, we are Welkom’s premier choice for advanced automotive electrical and security solutions. Modern vehicles rely heavily on complex electronic systems, and our mission is to provide the precise technical expertise needed to keep them functioning flawlessly. Based in Dagbreek, we combine cutting-edge diagnostic technology with years of hands-on experience to resolve everything from stubborn dashboard faults to complex key programming.

    Whether you need a spare car key coded, a high-tech security system installed, or a comprehensive electronic diagnosis, we handle your vehicle with the utmost care. We pride ourselves on offering efficient service, transparent pricing, and dependable call-outs when you need us most.

    Services

    Auto Electrical & Diagnostics

    Modern vehicles rely entirely on complex electrical systems. When a warning light pops up or your car refuses to start, our team uses state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment to pinpoint the exact problem without the guesswork.

  1. Full Computer Diagnostics & Scanning: Comprehensive system scans to detect engine, transmission, and electrical faults.
  2. Fault Finding & Rewiring: Repairing broken circuits, faulty fuses, damaged wiring harnesses, and alternators or starter motors.
  3. Convenient Call-Out Services: Stuck at home or on the side of the road? Our technical team can come directly to you in Welkom.

    4. Car Key Coding & Locksmith Services

    Losing your car keys or experiencing an electronic key malfunction can leave you stranded. We provide fast, dealership-grade car key replacements at a fraction of the price.

  4. Transponder Key Programming: Coding new keys, smart keys, and flip keys to sync perfectly with your vehicle’s computer box (ECU).
  5. Key Duplication & Spares: Cutting and coding high-quality spare keys for major vehicle brands including Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, BMW, Toyota, Honda, and more.
  6. Remote Repairs & Battery Replacement: Restoring non-responsive key fobs and programming central locking remotes.

    7. Vehicle Security & Specialised Installations

    Protect your investment with advanced vehicle security and custom electronic upgrades. We install industry-approved hardware to safeguard your car against theft.

  7. Anti-Theft Systems: Installation of robust alarms, engine immobilizers, and central locking upgrades.
  8. Car Tracking Devices: Fitting high-reliability tracking units to help monitor and protect your vehicle.
  9. Custom Sound & Audio Fitting: Professional installation of aftermarket car radios, speakers, amplifiers, and entertainment systems.

    10. Key & Remote Services:

    Car key programming, key coding, transponder keys, and spare keys for major brands (Volkswagen, Mercedes, Audi, BMW, Honda, etc.).

    Security & Specialized Installations:

    Immobilizers, central locking, car tracking devices, alarm systems, and custom sound fitting.

    Auto Electrical & Diagnostics:

    Full computer diagnostics (from R350), troubleshooting, and call-out services.
